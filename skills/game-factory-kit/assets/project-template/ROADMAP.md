# {{TITLE}} — Roadmap

## M0 — Core-loop prototype

Outcome: a player can start, perform the primary action, receive readable feedback, reach success or failure, and restart.

Exit gate:

- the complete loop works with placeholder content;
- the main state is understandable;
- the build has a documented launch route;
- the focused scenario in `QA.md` has been observed.

## M1 — Representative playable slice

Outcome: one thin path demonstrates the intended interaction and presentation quality.

Entry condition: M0 evidence identifies what is worth producing at higher quality.

## M2 — Public demo

Outcome: a new player can understand, complete, and restart a bounded experience without developer help.

Entry condition: the vertical slice and production pipeline are stable enough to expand.
